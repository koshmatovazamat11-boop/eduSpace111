# Usability checklist — EduSpace

This checklist maps design decisions to Nielsen's usability heuristics. It is a design review aid, not proof of usability. Validate with student testing.

1. **Visibility of system status** — course progress, XP, completed lessons and task states are visible.
2. **Match between system and real world** — familiar labels such as courses, lessons, notes and planner.
3. **User control and freedom** — close buttons, back navigation, editable notes and reversible task completion.
4. **Consistency and standards** — repeated buttons, cards, navigation and form patterns.
5. **Error prevention** — required fields, length limits and basic email validation in the demo profile.
6. **Recognition rather than recall** — course categories, lesson lists, progress indicators and clear labels.
7. **Flexibility and efficiency** — search, filters, keyboard-operable course cards and quick task entry.
8. **Aesthetic and minimalist design** — clear hierarchy, restrained palette, whitespace and focused page layouts.
9. **Help users recognize and recover from errors** — short validation and feedback messages.
10. **Help and documentation** — README, user flow and visible descriptions in key areas.

## Validation plan
Test with at least 5 students. Ask them to:
- Find a beginner course in a chosen subject.
- Enroll and complete one lesson.
- Create and find a note.
- Add and complete a planner task.
- Find their course progress.
Record task completion, time, errors and user comments. Fix repeated friction before adding more features.
